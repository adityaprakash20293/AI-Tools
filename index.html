AI Tools & Technologies

A practical guide to common AI tool categories and the kinds of work they can support. Explore use cases, selection considerations, and responsible-use checks.

Demo context: This is an independent, vendor-neutral learning aid. It describes tool categories rather than endorsing specific products. Capabilities, pricing, and data-handling terms vary by provider and change over time.
Research & knowledge

Research assistants

Support source discovery, document summarization, topic exploration, and synthesis of approved reference material.

Useful for: preparing SME interviews, summarizing reference documents, and building initial topic outlines.
Writing & content

Writing assistants

Help create and refine first drafts, adapt tone, simplify explanations, and structure content for different audiences.

Useful for: job aids, FAQs, communication drafts, and course scripts.
Visual & media

Visual and media tools

Assist with concept visuals, storyboards, narration, demonstrations, and other media assets.

Useful for: visual explanations, learning videos, and draft storyboards.
Workflow & productivity

Workflow assistants

Support repeatable tasks such as categorizing requests, extracting structured information, and drafting workflow steps.

Useful for: intake triage, content inventories, and process documentation.
